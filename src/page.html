How far to trust this map

Deck shapes, cabin positions, lift banks and stairs are taken from Royal Caribbean's own deck plan drawings. Venue names and positions were read off the cruisedeckplans.com deck images and placed by hand, so treat each venue as accurate to about 10 metres.

  • Distances are scaled from the plans using the ship's 347 m length. They are estimates for comparing routes, good to roughly 10 m.
  • Two lift banks appear on the plans: forward and aft. Every route here uses lifts only. The plans show the aft bank stopping at Deck 15.
  • The Solarium's Deck 13 entrance is not on the plans. It was added from a passenger's report and placed at the forward end of the Deck 13 corridor.
  • Deck 16 aft (FlowRider, RipCord, upper SeaPlex) is a separate island on the plans with only stairs drawn. Ask Guest Services whether a lift serves it.
  • Not on any deck plan: door widths, raised thresholds onto open decks, ramps, which toilets are accessible, pool hoists and wheelchair seating in venues. Check these on board on day one.
  • Venues change with refits. If a name here doesn't match the ship, the location is usually still the same space.
  • Port is the left side of the ship when you face the bow; starboard is the right. Even-numbered outside cabins numbered x1xx–x3xx are port, x5xx–x7xx starboard.
